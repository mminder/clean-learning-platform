## Goal

A customer cancels an upcoming appointment from the case detail. In time, the credit comes back; late, after a warning, it's forfeited. The appointment leaves the case, and its time is bookable again. Every count of open credits subtracts the forfeited ones.

## What to build

Read first: [UC-07](../../use-cases/uc-07-cancel-consultation-appointment.md), the case detail of [UC-05](../../use-cases/uc-05-view-my-purchases.md#main-flow-case-detail), [D-11](../../decisions.md#consultation), [D-27](../../decisions.md#architecture), the spec's decisions 1 to 4, 8, 11 and 12, its requirements [Cancel an appointment](../../specs/05-cancel-consultation-appointment-or-case.md#requirement-cancel-an-appointment) and [Open credits count forfeited credits](../../specs/05-cancel-consultation-appointment-or-case.md#requirement-open-credits-count-forfeited-credits), and the plan's [schema](README.md#schema), [API](README.md#api), [credits and completion](README.md#credits-and-completion), [cancelling an appointment](README.md#cancelling-an-appointment), [the case as its customer sees it](README.md#the-case-as-its-customer-sees-it), [the cancellation dialogs](README.md#the-cancellation-dialogs) and [tests](README.md#tests) sections. Increment 04's booking is the pattern for every layer: `BookConsultationAppointmentUseCase`, `CaseForBooking`, `ConsultationCaseBookingTest`, `BookAppointmentDialog` and `booking-failure.ts`.

Work in this order, test first:

1. **A failing REST test:** with the clock at Mon 9 Nov 2026, 15:00 (Zurich), Customer 1 buys Career Coaching and books Expert 2 on 17 Nov, 10:00–11:00. With the clock at Sun 15 Nov, 12:00, the case's upcoming appointment has `canCancel`, `freeCancellationUntil` 16 Nov, 10:00, and `creditRefundedIfCancelledNow`; `POST …/appointments/{appointmentId}/cancel` with `{"creditRefunded": true}` answers 200 with the case: 3 of 3 credits open, no appointment listed. The slots of Expert 2 offer 17 Nov, 10:00 again.
2. **Domain:** `Appointment`'s cancellation rules and `openCredits` with forfeited credits; `AppointmentCancellation`; `CaseForCancellation` with `cancelAppointment` and its codes; `CustomerUpcomingAppointment`; `CaseForBooking`, `CustomerConsultationCase` and `CustomerConsultationCaseSummary` count forfeited credits, all through `Appointment.openCredits`; the summary's Past key falls back to the purchase time (plan).
3. **Schema and adapter:** `V9__cancel_appointment.sql`; `StoredAppointmentStatus.CANCELLED` and `AppointmentEntity.creditRefunded`; `ConsultationCaseAdapter` implements `LockCaseForCancellationPort` and `SaveAppointmentCancellationPort`, and counts the forfeited credits for the detail, the list and the booking.
4. **Use case and API:** `CancelConsultationAppointmentUseCase` (lock, time, decide, write, answer); the route with `CancelConsultationAppointmentRequest`, `CustomerUpcomingAppointmentResponse` in the case detail; a role matrix row. Regenerate `openapi.json`, then `npm run generate:api`.
5. **Frontend:** `useCancelConsultationAppointment`; `cancellation-failure.ts` with the appointment's codes; `CancelAppointmentDialog`, rendered once by `ConsultationCasePage`; **Cancel** on each upcoming appointment with `canCancel`.
6. **Check by hand** on the dev server with Playwright: the dialog's texts in time and late (a 72-hour deadline makes a booking late from the start, as the spec's demo does), **Keep appointment**, the cancellation, the credits, and the freed time in the booking dialog; then cancel one appointment in two tabs, and see the second tab's message and reload.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] In time: 200, the credit is back, the appointment is no longer listed, and its time is offered again (REST).
- [x] Late (clock at 16 Nov, 11:00, `{"creditRefunded": false}`): 200, 2 of 3 credits open, and the appointment is no longer listed (REST).
- [x] At the deadline exactly the cancellation is late, and on both days of the summer-time changes the deadline is 24 real hours before the start (domain tests).
- [x] 409 `DEADLINE_PASSED` for `{"creditRefunded": true}` at 16 Nov, 10:01; 409 `APPOINTMENT_STARTED` at 17 Nov, 10:00; 409 `APPOINTMENT_CANCELLED` for a second cancellation; each with the plan's `detail`, and nothing changes (REST).
- [x] With every credit booked, a cancellation in time enables booking again (`canBook`); a case of 3 sessions with one consumed, one cancelled in time and one cancelled late has 1 open credit and lists only the consumed one (REST).
- [x] A case of 1 session whose appointment was cancelled late has 0 of 1 credits open and is `PAST` on **My purchases**, sorted by its purchase time (REST and domain).
- [x] A case of an archived offering can still cancel (REST).
- [x] Customer 2 cancelling Customer 1's appointment, and Customer 1 cancelling an appointment of another of their cases under this case's path, answer 404, and nothing changes; the role matrix has the route.
- [x] The dialog follows the plan's table and error table: it reloads its preview when it opens, names the appointment, says **Keep appointment** and **Cancel appointment**, stays open on a 409 and shows what's current, and a reload that removes the appointment doesn't close it (Playwright).
- [x] The case detail shows no free-cancellation time (spec decision 1) and no cancelled appointment (spec decision 3).
- [x] `openapi.json` and `schema.d.ts` have the new route and fields; every check passes.

## Notes

- **Beyond the plan, now in the plan:** `LockCaseForCancellationPort`'s method is `lockForCancellation`, not `lock`: `ConsultationCaseAdapter` already implements `LockCaseForBookingPort.lock(ConsultationCaseId, UserId)`, and Java can't have two methods with the same parameters and different return types on one class (as `LoadCustomerBusyTimesPort.loadCustomerBusyTimes` already does for the same reason). Task 03 uses the same method. `Appointment.sortedUpcoming(appointments, asOf)` selects and sorts as `upcoming` does but keeps the `Appointment`s, and `CustomerUpcomingAppointment.of(appointment, deadline, asOf)` builds the preview from `Appointment`'s three rules, so the preview has no copy of a rule. "Book your first session" shows only with `canBook`: after late cancellations forfeited every credit, it would sit next to a disabled **Book appointment** (plan's case detail and UC-05 updated).
- **The domain:** `Appointment.openCredits(sessionCount, booked, forfeited)` is the one rule; `CaseForBooking`, `CustomerConsultationCase` and `CustomerConsultationCaseSummary` each take `int forfeitedCredits` and call it, and `CaseForCancellation.forfeitedCredits()` counts its cancelled appointments without a refund (task 03 needs it for refund eligibility). `CaseForCancellation` refuses an appointment id that is both booked and cancelled (`IllegalArgumentException`), and its codes are constants next to `cancelAppointment`. `NotFoundException` for an appointment that is none of the case's comes from the domain, as the plan's table says; its detail is "Appointment <id> not found.". The summary's Past key is `pastOrderKey()`: the newest past appointment's start, or the purchase time; before, `lastAppointmentStart()` would have thrown for a Past case without an appointment (`getFirst()` on an empty list, a 500 on **My purchases**).
- **Persistence:** V8's inline check was named `appointment_status_check` (checked in the compose database, `pg_constraint`); V9 drops it and adds one of the same name with both statuses, and `appointment_credit_refunded_check`. On the compose database migrated to V8 first, V9 ran on start and left exactly these two checks besides `appointment_check`. The lock is the booking's `lockCustomerCaseEntity`: Hibernate sends `select … from consultation_case … where id = ? and customer_id = ? for no key update of cce1_0`, then `select … from appointment where consultation_case_id = ?` (every status). `save` changes the managed `AppointmentEntity` (found in the persistence context, no query); Hibernate writes `update appointment set …, credit_refunded = ?, …, status = ? where id = ?` when the answer's query of the booked appointments needs it (the answer's first query, the case's row, doesn't touch `appointment`, so it runs before the flush; both are in the SQL log). The forfeited credits are `count(a) … status = CANCELLED and creditRefunded = false` for the detail and the booking, and one grouped query into `ForfeitedCreditsRow` for the list. The booking's insert now names `credit_refunded` too, as null.

- **The dialog:** it has no query of its own: the page passes its case and the case query's `refetch`, so an opening sends one request (a second observer would refetch on mounting, too; in dev, StrictMode runs the effect twice, so two). Only **Cancel appointment** is disabled while the request runs; **Keep appointment** and Escape still close the dialog. From sending the request until it closes, the dialog shows the preview it sent: before the review, the reload that comes with the answer removed the appointment, and the closing dialog showed "This appointment can no longer be cancelled." as an alert for about 200 ms after a successful cancellation (review, checked with Playwright before and after).
